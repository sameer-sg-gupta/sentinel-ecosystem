READ THE MODEL CAREFULLY

Sentinel is a proposed ecosystem. The presentation does not establish guaranteed accident reduction, lower claims, premium savings, full systems integration, or autonomous action. Those are validation questions.

About Sentinel

Prevention should be visible.

Sentinel is a proposed neutral prevention-intelligence ecosystem. Its purpose is to make operational evidence more useful between inspections — connecting manufacturers, risk engineers, independent verification, and insurers around the same prevention record.
